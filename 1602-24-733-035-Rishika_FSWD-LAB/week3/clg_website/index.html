About the College

Vasavi College of Engineering is a premier engineering institution committed to providing quality technical education and developing skilled professionals.

The college provides an excellent academic environment with experienced faculty members, modern laboratories and facilities that encourage students to achieve academic and professional excellence.

The institution focuses on technical knowledge, innovation, research, entrepreneurship and overall development of students.

Quick Facts

Established: 1981
Location: Hyderabad
Affiliation: Osmania University
Type: Engineering College
Courses: UG & PG
